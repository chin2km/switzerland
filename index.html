Before you go

  • Check each morning: jungfrau.ch/en-gb/live/operating-info — the First gondola and Pfingstegg cable car stop in strong wind; All are in normal season (nothing closed for maintenance in September 2026).
  • Tickets: First (CHF 76) can be bought online at jungfrau.ch to skip the queue; Harder Kulm and Pfingstegg are bought on the spot. No pass is worth it for this plan.
  • Parking fills early at the First valley station on sunny days; fallback is the village car parks on Dorfstrasse (5 min walk). Lauterbrunnen Parkhaus is large and rarely full in September.
  • Fuel: ~975 km at 12 L/100 km is roughly 117 litres. Swiss petrol is around CHF 1.75/L; fill up before crossing into Switzerland where possible.
  • Long days: each day is 12–13 hours door to door with 4–4.5 h driving. Swap in an earlier drive home whenever the group is tired — everything after 15:00 on each day is optional.
  • Mürren alternative (free): if you skip the CHF 96 ride, walk the flat valley path from Lauterbrunnen to Stechelberg (6 km, 1h 20m) and take bus 141 back.
  • Day 4 timing: with a 9:00 check-out and Karlsruhe by ~16:00, Lucerne is a one-hour stretch-your-legs stop: Chapel Bridge and a loop of the Old Town squares. The Musegg Wall and Lion Monument were dropped — they'd add 45 min you don't have. If you'd rather skip Lucerne entirely, drive direct via Basel (~4h 15m) and arrive around 13:30. Fuel is cheaper in Germany — fill up after Basel, not before.
  • Maps: the embedded maps use Google Maps routing for every drive and walk, including the drive back to the Airbnb each evening; out-and-back walks show arrows in both directions and their direction button opens the full round trip. Mountain trails Google can't route follow hand-placed trail points instead. Cable cars and trains are drawn as straight dotted lines. Map tiles need internet — open the page once on Wi-Fi in the morning, or rely on the direction buttons which hand off to your maps app.
